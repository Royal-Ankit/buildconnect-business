Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains what personal information NIRMAZA collects, why we collect it and how it is protected. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and mobile number.
  • Requirements you send: the project details, location and budget you enter.
  • Business details (for professionals): business name, services, prices, service areas, photos and, if you choose, registration or certificate documents.
  • Technical data: basic device and usage information needed to keep the app secure and working.

2. How we use it

  • To create your account and show your listing or requirements.
  • To connect customers with the business they chose.
  • To verify businesses and prevent misuse.
  • To improve the app and support you when you contact us.

3. When contact details are shared

A customer's phone number and a business's contact details are shared with the other party only after the business accepts the requirement. Until then they stay private.

4. Documents

Certificates and registration documents are stored in a private area. Only you and our verification team can open them.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the other party in a requirement (as above), with service providers who host and run the app for us, or when the law requires.

6. Storage and security

Your data is stored with secure cloud providers and protected with access controls. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your password private.

7. Your choices

You can update your details in the app. You may ask us to correct or delete your account and data by contacting us. We process personal data in line with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

8. Children

The app is meant for adults. Please do not use it if you are under 18.

9. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above. Continued use means you accept the update.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy? See our Contact page.
