About NIRMAZA

Building Better Living

NIRMAZA is a construction-services marketplace for Uttar Pradesh. We help families and businesses find trusted architects, contractors, interior designers, carpenters, solar installers and other building professionals in one place, and we help those professionals reach customers who are ready to build.

For customersCompare verified businesses, see real project photos and prices, and send your requirement to the one vendor you choose.
For professionalsList your business free, show your work, set your own prices and decide which requirements to accept.
Privacy firstPhone numbers and contact details are shared only after a vendor accepts your requirement.

How it works

  • A customer picks one business and sends a requirement.
  • The business reads it and accepts or rejects it.
  • After acceptance, both sides see each other's contact details and continue directly.

Verification

Every business is reviewed by our team before it appears in listings. Businesses can add certificates and registration documents, which are stored privately.

Our promise

Simple, fair and transparent. We are a pilot platform and we are improving every week. If you have an idea or a problem, please contact us.
